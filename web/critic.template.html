Interview Question Critic

Paste the questions you plan to ask, one per line. This tells you which ones will produce answers you cannot use, before you spend an hour of someone else's time finding out.

It runs entirely in this page.

No account, no internet connection, no data sent anywhere. Save this file and it keeps working offline. Your questions never leave your browser.

What this cannot do.

Choosing what to ask about is the harder half, and it comes from writing down what you believe and then designing a question that could prove you wrong. This tool only checks how the question is put.
